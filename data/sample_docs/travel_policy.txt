================================================================================
VocaPulse Technologies — Business Travel & Expense Policy (FY2026)
================================================================================

1. MAXIMUM DAILY REIMBURSEMENT LIMITS & MEALS
Employees traveling on approved company business are eligible for a per diem meal reimbursement up to $85 USD per day in standard metropolitan areas, and up to $115 USD per day in designated high-cost tiers (e.g., New York City, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Zurich). Itemized receipts are mandatory for any single transaction exceeding $25 USD. Alcohol is reimbursed up to a maximum limit of $30 USD per day only when accompanied by a business meal with clients or external stakeholders.

2. FLIGHT BOOKINGS & AIR TRAVEL
All domestic commercial flights under 5 hours must be booked in Standard Economy or Premium Economy class. For non-stop international flights exceeding 7 consecutive flight hours, employees may book Business Class with prior VP approval. Flights must be booked through the company's Navan/Concur travel portal at least 14 days in advance to secure negotiated corporate rates.

3. HOTEL & LODGING ACCOMMODATIONS
The standard maximum hotel lodging rate is $220 USD per night (exclusive of local occupancy taxes) for domestic locations, and $280 USD per night for international tech hub locations. If hotel rates exceed these thresholds due to major conferences or peak seasons, written pre-approval from the Finance Director is required.

4. GROUND TRANSPORTATION & RIDESHARE
Employees should prioritize public transit, airport express trains, or standard rideshare options (UberX, Lyft Standard). Premium rideshare categories (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) and limousine rentals are strictly non-reimbursable unless accompanied by explicit executive authorization. Personal vehicle mileage for business travel is reimbursed at the current federal rate of $0.67 per mile.

5. EXPENSE SUBMISSION & APPROVAL WORKFLOW
All travel expense reports must be submitted within 21 calendar days following the completion of the trip. Failure to submit receipts and reports within 45 days may result in forfeiture of expense reimbursement.
