================================================================================
VocaPulse Pro Voice Hub & Smart Microphone — User & Hardware Manual (Model VP-900)
================================================================================

1. SPECIFICATIONS & SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
The VocaPulse VP-900 features an 8-beamforming microphone array with dynamic noise cancellation, real-time spatial acoustic filtering, and a dedicated NPU capable of 12 TOPS for on-device keyword spotting and acoustic preprocessing. 
- Frequency Response: 20 Hz – 20,000 Hz
- Acoustic Sensitivity: -26 dBFS ± 3 dB
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 6E (802.11ax), Bluetooth 5.3 LE, and USB-C 3.2 Gen 2 (10 Gbps)
- Power Input: USB-PD 45W Type-C adapter or internal 6000 mAh rechargeable lithium-ion battery offering up to 14 hours of continuous voice querying.

2. HARDWARE SETUP & PAIRING PROCEDURE
Step 1: Connect the supplied 45W USB-C cable to the power port on the base of the VP-900.
Step 2: Press and hold the multi-function Power button on the rear panel for 3 seconds until the 360-degree LED halo pulses soft emerald green.
Step 3: Open the VocaRAG Desktop Suite or mobile companion app, select "Add Device", and confirm the 6-digit pairing code shown on your host screen.
Step 4: Calibration runs automatically for 15 seconds, measuring room reverberation and ambient noise profiles.

3. LED RING STATUS INDICATORS
- Solid Emerald Green: System ready, listening mode active.
- Pulsing Amber: Microphone muted via physical hardware privacy switch.
- Fast Flashing Magenta: Network handshake in progress or firmware update OTA downloading.
- Solid Crimson Red: Thermal warning or hardware fault detected (device temperature > 55°C).

4. TROUBLESHOOTING & FACTORY RESET
If the microphone array fails to register speech input:
1. Verify that the physical privacy shutter on top of the device is toggled to Open.
2. Ensure no USB hub or unpowered splitter is placed between the device and host computer.
3. Perform a Hard Reset: Insert a paperclip into the Pin-hole Reset slot on the bottom plate for 10 seconds until all LEDs flash white three times.
